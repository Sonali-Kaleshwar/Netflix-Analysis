## Overview
This report provides a comprehensive analysis of Netflix’s content, focusing on Movies and TV Shows. The analysis uses data visualized through an interactive Power BI dashboard to uncover trends, insights, and key performance indicators (KPIs). The findings can help Netflix identify popular genres, countries with high audience engagement, and overall content performance.
### KPI's Questions
#### 1. Key Metrics

Total Number of Titles

Average Ratings

Total Votes

Votes per Title

#### 2. Questions to Solve:

What are the average ratings for Movies vs. TV Shows?

Which countries contribute the most titles and votes?

Which genres have the highest average ratings and title counts?

How do user votes correlate with ratings and content popularity?

What are the regional trends in content engagement?

### Steps Followed
1. Data Cleaning:

- Imported and cleaned the dataset in Power BI.

- Removed duplicates and missing data where necessary.

- Standardized column names for consistency.

2. Data Transformation:

- Used Power Query for transformations (e.g., extracting genres, splitting movies and TV shows, and categorizing rating groups).

- Created calculated columns and measures using DAX.

3. Visualizations:

- Pyramid chart to display the count of titles by rating groups.

- Bar charts to visualize genres by average ratings and title count.

- Geo-map to highlight the number of titles and votes per country.

- Table with detailed insights on individual titles (e.g., ratings, votes, and plot summaries).

### Insights

#### Content Distribution by Rating:

- Majority of titles fall within the 6-8 rating range.

- Movies have an average rating of 6.12, while TV shows perform better with an average rating of 7.18.

#### Regional Trends:

- United States has the highest number of titles (2,355) and total votes (7.8M votes).

- Other countries like the United Kingdom, Japan, and South Korea also contribute significantly to the content library.

#### Genre Performance:

- Genres like Documentary and Biography have higher average ratings (above 7.0).

- Popular genres by title count include Drama, Comedy, and Thriller, but their average ratings vary.

#### Engagement:

- High correlation between the number of votes and popularity.

- Certain low-rated genres like Horror still receive substantial engagement, indicating niche audience preferences.

#### Visualization Insights:

- The geo-map clearly shows North America, Europe, and Asia as key regions for both content production and audience engagement.

### Final Conclusion

The Power BI dashboard provides actionable insights into Netflix’s content library. TV Shows outperform Movies in terms of average ratings, while genres like Documentary and Biography receive better reception. The United States dominates in terms of content and audience votes, but significant contributions also come from other regions. The analysis highlights opportunities to improve content performance by focusing on underperforming genres and leveraging regions with high engagement for strategic content production.

##### The findings can be used by Netflix’s content and strategy teams to:

- Optimize their content library.

- Target specific regions and genres.

- Enhance audience engagement by prioritizing highly rated genres.
